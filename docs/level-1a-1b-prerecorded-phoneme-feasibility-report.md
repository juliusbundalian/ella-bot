# Feasibility Report: Prerecorded Phoneme Activities (Levels 1A & 1B)

**Project:** E.L.L.A. Offline Reading Assistant  
**Date:** 29 July 2026  
**Subject:** Feasibility of prerecorded phoneme audio with automated learner-response checking

---

## 1. Executive Summary

This report evaluates replacing text-to-speech (TTS) with prerecorded human audio for Level 1A (vowels) and Level 1B (consonants), combined with automated response checking. 

While playing prerecorded human audio is fully feasible, **automated grading of isolated phonemes is currently unreliable**. Speech recognizers require word context and fail on isolated phonemes, leading to high false-rejection and false-acceptance rates. Furthermore, no labeled learner dataset exists to train or validate an offline phoneme classifier.

**Recommendation:** Remove Levels 1A and 1B from the automated assessment pipeline until a validated phoneme evaluation method and representative learner dataset are available.

---

## 2. Background & Problem Statement

- **Level 1A & 1B Goal:** Teach isolated vowel and consonant sounds.
- **TTS Limitation:** TTS models struggle with isolated consonants, often appending unwanted vowel sounds (e.g., pronouncing /b/ as "buh").
- **Proposed Fix:** Use human-recorded audio clips for lesson demonstrations.

---

## 3. Proposed Activity Workflow

1. **Demonstration:** ELLA plays a prerecorded human phoneme audio clip. *(Feasible)*
2. **User Input:** Learner repeats the sound into the microphone. *(Feasible)*
3. **Evaluation:** ELLA processes and grades the phoneme response. *(Unreliable)*
4. **Feedback:** ELLA displays results and records progress. *(Unreliable due to Step 3)*

---

## 4. Key Technical Findings

1. **Playback is Fully Feasible:** Human audio clips significantly improve demonstration clarity over TTS.
2. **ASR Fails Without Word Context:** Current ASR (Vosk) matches spoken audio against vocabulary words. Isolated phonemes (lasting milliseconds) lack context, causing ASR to output random words or no response.
3. **High Sensitivity to Audio Noise:** Microphone variations, room noise, child pitch, and accent differences distort isolated phoneme evaluation.
4. **Lack of Labeled Dataset:** Developing a custom offline phoneme classifier requires a large dataset of labeled children's voice recordings, which is not currently available.

---

## 5. Options Evaluated

| Option | Approach | Feasibility | Recommendation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A. Human Audio + Current ASR** | Play human audio; grade using existing ASR. | High Playback / Low Grading | **Not Recommended** (Unreliable grading). |
| **B. Expanded Word Matching** | Accept word approximations generated by ASR. | Medium | **Not Recommended** (High false-positive rate). |
| **C. Custom Phoneme Model** | Train an offline ML phoneme classifier. | High Potential | **Future Phase** (Requires dataset). |
| **D. Teacher Manual Review** | Store audio clips for manual grading by teachers. | High | **Optional** (Requires storage & privacy compliance). |

---

## 6. Risks of Proceeding

- **False Negatives:** Learner speaks correctly but is marked wrong, causing frustration.
- **False Positives:** Incorrect pronunciation is accepted, reinforcing bad habits.
- **Unreliable Metrics:** Stored progress logs fail to reflect actual reading ability.

---

## 7. Recommendations & Next Steps

1. **Remove Levels 1A & 1B** from the automated assessment flow.
2. **Focus Assessment on Level 1C+** (syllables, words, phrases, and sentences where ASR performs reliably).
3. **Reconsider 1A/1B in a Future Phase** once:
   - A consented children's phoneme dataset is collected.
   - An offline phoneme evaluation model is benchmarked.
   - An "Uncertain / Try Again" score state is introduced.

---

## 8. Conclusion

Prerecorded audio solves lesson demonstration clarity but does not solve automated grading for isolated phonemes. Removing Levels 1A and 1B ensures ELLA maintains high assessment fairness and accuracy.
